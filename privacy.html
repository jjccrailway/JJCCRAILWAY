Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how JJCC Railways handles information when you use this railway reservation website. JJCC Railways is an academic project. Please provide only the information needed to use the website’s features.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account information: Your name and email address, if you create an account.
  • Booking information: Your booking reference, route, travel date, passenger details, fare, and booking status.
  • Payment and wallet information: Your selected payment method, transaction status, any receipt you upload, and your JCoins balance or transaction history. Never provide your bank password, card PIN, or account login credentials.
  • Support and request information: Details you provide when contacting support, requesting a refund, or applying for delay compensation.
  • Technical information: Basic device, browser, and website activity information that may be processed by the website or its hosting and database providers.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, manage reservations and trip details, verify payments, maintain booking and wallet records, review eligible refund or delay-compensation requests, provide service notices, and maintain the website’s security and functionality.

3. Service providers and sharing

JJCC Railways may rely on third-party services, such as Supabase for authentication and database functions and a hosting provider to make the website available. These providers may process information as needed to operate the website. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or if necessary to protect the website, its users, or their rights.

4. Data retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to manage accounts, reservations, transactions, support requests, security, or project demonstrations. We use access controls and reasonable technical safeguards, but cannot guarantee that a website or online transmission will always be completely secure.

5. Your choices and requests

Please keep your account information accurate and protect your sign-in credentials. You may contact the JJCC Railways team to ask about your personal information or request that it be corrected or deleted. We may need to retain some records for transaction integrity, security, or legal reasons.

6. Children’s privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, please contact the team so we can review the matter.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website’s features or project requirements change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the Contact us page.
