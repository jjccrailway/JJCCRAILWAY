Travel Information & Policies

Booking, payment, refunds, delay compensation and passenger guidelines for JJCC Railways

  • Regular passenger bookings are held for 10 minutes while the passenger completes payment and uploads a receipt.
  • Student, Senior Citizen, and PWD discount claims require a valid ID upload and administrator verification. Seats remain reserved while ID verification is pending; there is no automatic ID-review expiry.
  • After a discount ID is approved, the passenger receives an in-app notification and has 10 minutes from notification time to upload the payment receipt.
  • If a discount ID is rejected, the passenger is automatically changed to Regular (no discount), and the 10-minute payment countdown starts when the rejection notification is received.
  • A booking is confirmed only after the submitted payment receipt has been reviewed and approved.
  • Payment receipt review is a manual verification step. Uploading a receipt alone does not confirm that funds have been received.
  • JCoins are valued at 1 JCoin = PHP 1. JCoins are credited to your account wallet and are not redeemable for cash.
  • Voluntary cancellation is available within 24 hours after payment confirmation. The 24-hour period starts from the booking's payment approval timestamp, not from booking creation.
  • Eligible voluntary cancellations receive 50% of the original ticket fare as JCoins.
  • The booking is cancelled immediately and the refund is credited to the passenger's JCoins Wallet automatically. No administrator approval is required.
  • After the 24-hour window, voluntary cancellation with a refund is not available.
  • If JJCC Railways cancels a confirmed journey, 100% of the original ticket fare is credited to the passenger's JCoins Wallet.
  • JCoins cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for cash. JCoins do not expire.

Delay is measured by comparing the verified actual arrival time at the passenger's destination with the scheduled arrival time.

  • Below 30 minutes: no compensation.
  • 30–59 minutes: 25% of the original ticket fare.
  • 60–119 minutes: 50% of the original ticket fare.
  • 120 minutes or more: 100% of the original ticket fare.
  • Passengers may submit an optional Delay Compensation Request and enter the actual arrival date and time they experienced.
  • An Admin or Super Admin verifies the passenger-reported arrival time, then approves or rejects each request.
  • JCoins are credited to the passenger's wallet only after Admin approval. Recording a delay alone does not issue compensation.
  • Only one compensation request is allowed per booking. The passenger-reported time is not treated as verified until Admin review.
  • Regular passengers must upload their payment receipt within 10 minutes after booking creation.
  • Discounted passengers do not have a payment countdown while their ID is pending verification. If approved, their 10-minute countdown starts when the approval notification is sent. If rejected, the passenger becomes Regular and the 10-minute countdown starts when the rejection notification is sent.
  • If no receipt is submitted before an individual passenger’s deadline, only that passenger’s seat is released. Other passengers in the same booking remain reserved.
  • If a receipt is submitted on time, the payment remains pending review until an Admin or Super Admin reviews it. An administrator review delay does not expire or cancel a submitted payment or release that passenger’s seat.
  • If a submitted receipt is rejected, any JCoins used for that passenger are returned and a new 10-minute receipt upload window starts when the rejection notification is sent.
  • Flexible fares can be changed without a change fee before departure, subject to availability and any fare difference.
  • Saver fares may require a change fee and any fare difference.
  • Each booking is eligible for one combined modification request under the booking-change rules.
  • Any modification fee is non-refundable. A change is not complete until the required payment and review steps have been completed.
  • Each passenger may bring one large bag (up to 23 kg) and one carry-on bag (up to 7 kg).
  • Keep your bags with you, or store them in the luggage racks at the end of each coach.
  • Extra bags may be carried subject to space and a small handling fee at the station.
  • Folded bicycles are carried free. Full-size bicycles need a reservation on services with bike space.
  • Sports equipment, musical instruments and similar items must fit in the luggage area without blocking aisles.
  • Items heavier than 30 kg or longer than 1.5 m may not be carried.
  • Carry a valid government-issued ID. The name on your ticket must match your ID.
  • Student, Senior Citizen, and PWD passengers receive a 20% discount only after their uploaded matching ID is approved by an Admin or Super Admin. Accepted upload formats are JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, and PDF (maximum 5 MB).
  • If a discount ID is rejected, the passenger type automatically changes to Regular and the fare is recalculated without the discount.
  • Show your e-ticket on your phone or as a printout. Keep it until you leave the destination station.
  • Small pets travel free in a secure, ventilated carrier that stays on your lap or under your seat.
  • Guide and assistance dogs travel free and may sit with their handler on all services.
  • Pets may not occupy seats. Passengers are responsible for their animals at all times.
  • Request boarding assistance at least 24 hours before travel through Customer Service.
  • Wheelchair spaces and priority seats are available in designated coaches.
  • Station staff can help with boarding, alighting and transfers at all JJCC stations.
  • Be considerate: keep noise low, and give priority seats to those who need them.
  • Smoking and vaping are not allowed on trains or in enclosed station areas.
  • Dangerous or prohibited items are not allowed. Staff may ask passengers to leave if safety is at risk.
